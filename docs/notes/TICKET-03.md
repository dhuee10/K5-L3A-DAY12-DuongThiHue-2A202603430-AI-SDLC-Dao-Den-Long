# TICKET-03 context (Phase 3): Change Request "Đèn cạn dầu" · Contract v1.2

Goal changes from "light them all" to "keep the island lit until dawn". Rules marked [REPLACES] override Ticket-02. ADR + updated tests + breaking-change changelog are required. Creative extras stay, but must follow the new rules and must not change headless output.

1. **Lantern fuel [REPLACES "a lit lantern takes no more motes"]:** max 100; lighting or relighting sets fuel = 100; a lit lantern burns 1/s (1/60 per tick). Refuel = interact at a lit lantern: `used = min(carried, ceil((100 − fuel)/10))`, `fuel = min(100, fuel + 10·used)`, minus `used` motes. A full lantern (used = 0) cannot be picked, emits nothing and scores nothing. Interact picks among receivable lanterns only (unlit, or lit and not full): nearest, tie → smaller id. Track fuel in integer 1/60 units; round only on output. Fuel ≤ 0 → lantern goes out, `deposited = 0`, event `lantern_out`, ambient light drops, `ever_lit = true` is kept. Relighting an `ever_lit` lantern costs 5; never-lit still costs 10. Display: fuel bar on lit lanterns; below 30 the lantern and its bar blink slowly (1 Hz).
2. **Shades drain fuel [REPLACES "shades never enter the safe zone"]:** a lantern with fuel < 30 loses its safe zone (exactly 30 still has it). Only `wander` shades choose a weak lantern: the nearest lit one with fuel < 30 (tie → smaller id); if there is none, they go to the nearest unlit lantern as before. `chase` shades keep their target. Within 2 m (XZ) → new state `drain`: stand still, drain 5 fuel/s (5/60 per tick); several shades add up, and normal burn continues. Leaving `drain`: → `chase` if Mây comes within 6 m; → `stunned` if pulsed; → `wander` if the lantern goes out, is refuelled to ≥ 30, or the shade is > 2 m away. After a stun → `wander`. Refuelling to ≥ 30 with shades inside 6 m pushes them to the edge within ≤ 30 ticks, with no damage meanwhile (same as lighting).
3. **New win rule [REPLACES "win as soon as all are lit"]:** nights last 240 / 270 / 300 s. Win = alive at dawn with lit lanterns on the last tick ≥ ceil(0.6 × total) (3/5, 4/6, 5/7). No early win. Lose = no flames (immediately, even on the last tick) or dawn below the threshold. Reasons: `dawn_survived` (win), `dawn` / `no_flames` (lose). Tick order: in stage 2 also subtract fuel (burn + drain); right after stage 2, lanterns with fuel ≤ 0 go out. Win/lose is checked after everything else in the tick; no flames → `no_flames` first.
4. **Growing capacity:** capacity = 10 + 2 × successful_lights, max 20. Counts every successful light in the night, including relights (scenario pre-lit lanterns don't count). Resets each night. HUD shows carried / capacity.
5. **New scoring [REPLACES all]:** +5 per mote put into a lantern (light, relight or refuel); +100 first light; +40 relight; pulse hit +5 per shade, or +15 if it was draining; at dawn (win only): per lit lantern +50 + floor(fuel), per flame +50. Time bonus removed. Game total and replay unchanged.
6. **Colour-blind mode (saved setting):** shade rim + eyes #7A3CFF → #00B3FF; UI warning #FF5A5F → #FFFFFF plus ⚠; unlit lanterns 20 % squashed with torn paper; low-fuel lanterns blink.

Snapshot: lantern `fuel` (1 decimal, 0 when out), `ever_lit`, `relit_count`; `player.capacity`; shade state `drain`.
